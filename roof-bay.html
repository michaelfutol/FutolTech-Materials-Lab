M2 · roof bay geometry

One roof plane between two rafters

This first M2 build distributes roof pressure to each purlin using explicit tributary widths, solves every purlin as a simply supported C-section between two rafters, then transfers reactions into the two rafter lines. Roof-sheet, screw and rafter member capacities are intentionally unresolved.

Permanent boundary

Current C-purlin response is gross-section elastic screening. Local/distortional/LTB, roof-sheet capacity, screw pull-out/pull-over, purlin-to-rafter connection capacity, rafter/truss member design and code wind zoning remain separate future checks.
