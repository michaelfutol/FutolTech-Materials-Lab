Engineering boundary

This is a gross-section elastic educational screen. Wind pressures are user-supplied net design pressures; this bench does not calculate NSCP pressure coefficients or roof zones. Any parapet, enclosure/internal-pressure, roof-zone and building-envelope effects must therefore already be reflected in the net wind pressures supplied to this bench. Effective width, local/distortional buckling, lateral-torsional behavior, diaphragm restraint, screw/weld/cleat strength, fatigue and nonlinear post-yield behavior remain outside this module.

Equation basis / references

Simply supported uniform-load formulas: Mmax = wL²/8 and δmax = 5wL⁴/(384EI); elastic bending stress σ = M/Z; vector resolution uses elementary statics/trigonometry; 1 kPa·m = 1 kN/m. These are standard Strength/Mechanics of Materials relations (e.g. Hibbeler; Beer, Johnston et al.; Roark). Exact cold-formed design must use the governing code and verified section/product data.

Engineering notice: This visualization teaches load path and elastic response. Verify code-derived wind pressures, load combinations, actual section geometry/grade, roof restraint, fasteners, welds and all governing cold-formed limit states before design use.
