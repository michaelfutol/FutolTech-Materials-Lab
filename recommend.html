What “sufficient” means here

Wood uses the selected allowable bending reference plus deflection. Steel uses first yield plus deflection. Commercial-length limits are also checked; a longer member is marked as needing a splice.

Optimizer boundary

This first solver enumerates every listed candidate and verifies it classically. It is not yet a cost optimizer, connection designer, local-buckling check, or quantum solver. Later QUBO searches may choose members, braces, joints, stock lengths, and splices together; the classical solver must still verify the selected solution.

Engineering notice: Recommendations are preliminary analytical comparisons using the current source datasets and idealised beam model. Verify actual materials, sections, connections, stability, loads, and governing codes before design use.
