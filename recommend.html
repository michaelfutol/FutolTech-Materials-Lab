What “sufficient” means here

Coconut wood uses its published proposed allowable reference. Other Philippine woods currently use published research references, not code design allowables. Kawayan-tinik uses the study’s suggested permissible bending stress for quality-controlled mature culms. Steel uses first yield plus deflection. Stock or usable straight length is checked only where a defensible boundary is defined.

Optimizer boundary

The glowing option is the minimum under the selected objective—not automatically the cheapest item in pesos. Pipe, SHS/RHS, and rolled sections are now classified separately. Bamboo is modeled as a hollow round culm; taper, ovality, nodes, splitting, treatment, durability, and joint behavior remain separate checks.

Engineering notice: Recommendations are preliminary analytical comparisons using source datasets and an idealised beam model. Verify actual materials, species, grading, dimensions, moisture, treatment, connections, stability, loads, legality, market availability, and governing codes before design use.
