Solution-package rule

A strong member is not automatically a complete solution. If the required length exceeds verified stock length, Design Explorer creates a minimum stock-piece planning bound and marks the splice connection REQUIRED / UNVERIFIED. Connection Lab v1 research screening is not promoted into a design PASS.

Price and carbon are not ranked until verified supplier-price and product EPD/carbon data exist. Missing commercial data is shown as unavailable rather than estimated.

Design Explorer boundary

MEMBER FEASIBLE means the implemented member checks and verified stock-length boundary are satisfied without a splice; it is not a whole-detail structural PASS because end/support connections remain outside the package. INCOMPLETE means a member may work but a required stock/splice/connection dependency is unresolved. SCREENING is reserved for section families whose governing physics are not yet complete.

The Pareto marker means no other currently eligible alternative is simultaneously no worse in purchased mass, governing utilisation, splice count and stock waste while being strictly better in at least one. It is not a price or carbon optimum.

Engineering notice: Design Explorer outputs are preliminary analytical solution packages. Verify exact species/grade, dimensions, stability, stock, splices, connections, supplier availability, prices, environmental data, loads and governing codes before design use.
