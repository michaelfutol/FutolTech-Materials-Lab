What “sufficient” means here

Coco uses its published proposed allowable reference. Yakal, Guijo, Molave/Tugas, Narra, Apitong, Red Lauan, White Lauan, Tanguile, and big-leaf Mahogany use provisional historical Philippine working-stress rows pending reconciliation against the project timber book. Kawayan-tinik uses its study permissible reference. Steel uses first yield plus deflection.

Because verified density is still missing for the common hardwoods, they can be compared by strength, deflection, and utilisation but are not eligible to win a lowest-mass ranking yet. Ipil remains library-only pending a defensible property package.

Optimizer boundary

The glowing option is the minimum under the selected objective—not automatically the cheapest item in pesos. Local QUBO mode formulates a one-member binary selection problem with exactly-one and feasibility penalties, exhaustively solves its small binary window, and sends the chosen member back through deterministic structural checks.

Engineering notice: Recommendations are preliminary analytical comparisons. Verify exact species, visual grade, legal origin, dimensions, moisture, treatment, defects, connections, stability, loads, market availability, and governing codes before design use.
