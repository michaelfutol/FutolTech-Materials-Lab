What this version can and cannot do

Useful now: distributed load build-up, equal grid generation, actual continuous-joist reactions into bearers, bearer reactions into shores, reaction balance, spacing sensitivity, elastic joist/bearer comparison, and individual-shore compression/global-buckling screening.

Not construction-ready: plywood capacity, local concrete piles and pump impact, simple-span/splice alternatives, shear and bearing, nails/clamps/wedges/U-heads/base plates, steel local buckling, timber grading and defects, shore settlement, brace strength/stiffness, soil or supporting-slab capacity, pour sequence, stripping, reshoring and multilevel construction-load distribution.

Engineering notice: SH-001 is a preliminary screening and layout tool—not a release-for-pour design. A qualified engineer must verify the full formwork/shoring system, drawings, actual materials, connections, bracing, foundations and construction sequence.
