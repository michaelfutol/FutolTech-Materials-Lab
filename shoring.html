Current engineering boundary

SH-001 checks an idealized slab-to-joist-to-bearer-to-shore load path. It includes fresh concrete, editable rebar and construction allowances, plywood self-weight, optional framing self-weight, continuous joist/bearer elastic analysis, shore reactions, global flexural buckling screening and variable brace levels. It does not yet verify plywood panel bending, local concrete heaping or pump impact, joist/bearer bearing, nails/clamps/wedges/U-heads/base plates, shore settlement, damaged or reused members, brace strength/stiffness, soil/supporting-slab capacity, pour sequence, stripping, reshoring or multilevel load distribution.

Engineering notice: Temporary works are safety-critical. This module is for preliminary comparison and layout study only. A qualified engineer must verify the complete shoring/formwork system, construction sequence, actual materials, connections, foundations and governing requirements before use.
