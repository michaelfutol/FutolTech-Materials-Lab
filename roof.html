The tab checks the selected rafter under the generated downward joist reactions. It does not yet design the joists, roof sheathing, purlins, nails/bolts/straps, bearing, uplift, lateral-torsional stability, diaphragm action, complete truss action, or support foundations. Wind uplift must be analysed as a separate load case with a verified continuous load path.
