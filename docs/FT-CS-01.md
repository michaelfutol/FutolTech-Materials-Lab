# FT-CS-01 — Engineering Calculation Sheet

Primary company identity: **FUTOLTECH ENGINEERING AND PROJECT SYSTEMS**  
Engineer: **MICHAEL D FUTOL, RCE, RMP**

## Purpose

FT-CS-01 is the default interior-page standard for Materials Lab reports, structural calculations, comparison sheets, technical appendices, and analysis summaries.

## Print architecture

- A4 landscape for Structural Member Comparison.
- Explicit page containers instead of fixed browser-print overlays.
- In-flow header, content region, and footer on every page.
- Compact continuation headers after page 1.
- No repeating long disclaimer.
- Preliminary-engineering limitations appear once in the final notes area.
- Normal technical body text targets 10.5–11 pt, with smaller metadata only where needed.
- Safe internal margins prioritize readability, annotations, and reliable PDF output.

## Visual language

- White background.
- Charcoal technical text.
- Restrained deep-navy accent.
- Thin rules and disciplined grid alignment.
- No gradients, swooshes, oversized logos, generic AI card grids, or brochure-style decoration.

This standard belongs to the four-template FutolTech document family: FT-LH-01, FT-CS-01, FT-TR-01, and FT-CV-01.
