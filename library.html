How to read the library

GI pipe means a locally marketed galvanized round pipe listed under a pipe standard such as PNS 26 or ASTM A53. SHS/RHS means structural hollow section. Galvanizing is a coating, not a steel grade.

The primary timber set now includes Coco, Yakal, Guijo, Molave/Tugas, Narra, Apitong, Red Lauan, White Lauan, Tanguile, and big-leaf Mahogany. Their working-stress rows remain provisional until reconciled with the project timber book and verified for exact species, grade and moisture.

Ipil and lumber sold only under the ambiguous trade label “Philippine mahogany” remain pending. Visual sketches are cross-section diagrams, not fabrication drawings.
