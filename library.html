How to read the library

GI pipe means a locally marketed galvanized round pipe listed under a pipe standard such as PNS 26 or ASTM A53. SHS/RHS means structural hollow section. Galvanizing is a coating, not a steel grade.

The Common local timbers view keeps Apitong, Yakal, Red Lauan, White Lauan, Tanguile and Narra easy to find. They remain inactive in calculations until exact species, legal origin, grade, moisture and structural properties are verified.

Visual sketches are cross-section diagrams, not fabrication drawings. Actual dimensions, grade certificates, species, moisture, treatment, legality, and stock availability still require verification.
