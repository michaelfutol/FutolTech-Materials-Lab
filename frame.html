NF-001 engineering boundary

NF-001 is a software/physics benchmark, not a code-designed wall. Member E/A/I are elastic inputs. A rigid or pin joint is an explicit idealization; a semi-rigid joint exists only when you provide kθ. P–Δ is an elastic geometric-stiffness iteration. Connection degradation uses only an explicit moment threshold and residual-stiffness ratio and is not inferred from nails, bolts, straps, welds, or workmanship. Brace Adviser v1 compares elastic stiffness and axial demand only; it does not claim brace or brace-connection capacity.
